# Kacha brand assets

- `kacha-logo.png` is the user-supplied source logo.
- `kacha-og.png` is the official social card derived from that logo for the
  Kacha website and repository.

These files are product identity assets, not implementation examples. Their
usage rules are defined in
[`docs/PRODUCT_BRAND_AND_WEBSITE.md`](../../docs/PRODUCT_BRAND_AND_WEBSITE.md).

The brand assets are excluded from the repository's MIT license. They may be
displayed when referring to Kacha, linking to this repository, or showing an
unmodified installation of the project. They may not be used to imply
endorsement, impersonate the project, or create a confusingly similar product
identity.
